## Activity classification

**A — proceed:** source reading; metadata discovery; review of existing QA; literature and reference-data research; documentation/security/storage/environment audits; proposed team/workflow arrangements; written future architecture/evaluation/backlog. No new solution components. Skills are documented rather than demonstrated through project-specific code.

**B — pause execution:** new project-specific preprocessing; loaders; reusable processing utilities; executable API/GDAL pipelines; polished UI designs; completed demos; prototypes. Existing crops, masks, external QA utilities and detailed prior plans are preserved and flagged for organizer review, not deleted or presented as event-built work. Category B reuse remains unauthorized until clarified.

**C — not permitted yet:** competition algorithms/models, frontend/backend/endpoints, production processing, database/dashboard, AI/report engine and integrated demo. Gate: verified Ahmedabad official build start, applicable permission, AND owner phrase `START TERRASCOPE AI HACKATHON BUILD`. Calendar date alone is insufficient.

The global FAQ finding increases eligibility uncertainty about earlier project-specific preparation. This review does not certify that existing artifacts are eligible. Keep source records, seek an authoritative ruling, and rebuild restricted artifacts during the event if instructed. See [organizer draft](AHMEDABAD_CLARIFICATION.md).
