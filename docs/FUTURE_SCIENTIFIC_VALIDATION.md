# Future scientific validation plan — not implemented

The current pair is a candidate dataset, not an established before-flood/after-flood pair. Complete the following only within applicable event rules. Competition code requires verified local event start and explicit `START TERRASCOPE AI HACKATHON BUILD` authorization.

1. **Confirm event and reference dates first.** Obtain contemporaneous Majuli observations near each exact UTC acquisition. Inspect [ASDMA daily situation reports](https://asdma.assam.gov.in/information-services/detail/assam-flood-report) and [NRSC/NDEM products](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/), but confirm acquisition dates, coverage, map legends and independence. District reports establish context, not pixel truth. A flood elsewhere in Assam does not establish flooding inside this AOI.
2. **Verify geometry and radiometry.** Check stable features for physical offsets, evaluate incidence/terrain, shadow/layover/static information and radiometric banding. Noise correction was not applied. Review source noise/calibration LUTs before treating dark values near the noise floor as physical change. Do not arbitrarily subtract noise, normalize dates or apply generic thresholds. Preserve linear gamma0 originals and provenance; document any later conversion, masking or resampling.
3. **Build independent validation evidence.** Use cloud/shadow-screened optical observations where available, such as [Sentinel-2 products](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/sentinel-data-access/sentinel-products/sentinel-2-data-products) or [USGS Landsat Level-2](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-level-2-science-products). Verify regional availability: do not assume the ready-made Landsat DSWE product covers Assam. A second SAR sensor such as [Sentinel-1](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s1-applications) offers corroboration, but shares radar-related ambiguities and a different wavelength. Coarse NASA flood products support context, not 10 m pixel truth. No matching reference imagery or ground truth has yet been acquired or validated.
4. **Separate hypotheses from confirmed flooding.** Calm open water may be dark; flooded vegetation may brighten through double bounce. Wet soil, vegetation growth, harvesting, sandbars, river migration, wind-roughened water and agricultural changes can also alter backscatter. Persistent water is not new floodwater. Use baseline seasonality and independent timing to explain alternatives. Assess the known P05023 joint-mode 40 MHz banding caveat before interpreting spatial patterns.
5. **Evaluate on held-out, interpretable evidence.** After a future method exists, report a confusion matrix, precision `TP/(TP+FP)`, recall `TP/(TP+FN)`, F1 `2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)` and IoU `TP/(TP+FP+FN)`. Report undefined denominators rather than invented scores. Include sample sizes, missing/cloud-obscured regions, time offsets, boundary uncertainty and spatially separated validation samples. Record area bias against independent references with resolution-matched masks; do not rely solely on overall accuracy for rare flood pixels. If no credible reference exists, label outputs unvalidated backscatter-change hypotheses and omit accuracy claims.

These are planning estimates, not downloads or guarantees. Ask before any planned >1 GB transfer or unexpectedly large operation.

Prefer alternative observations if this pair lacks a documented event transition, meaningful mode metadata cannot be clarified, banding/noise or physical offsets dominate the intended measurement, or independent references are unavailable. Prefer matching track/frame/mode and processing maturity/version; do not combine BETA/PROVISIONAL differences as environmental change.
