# Scientific and AI responsibility

TerraScope AI is preparation for a research prototype, not an operational disaster-response or warning system. Do not advise evacuation, certify safety or claim agency endorsement from exploratory results.

Every scientific claim should state its evidence, date, units, method, quality exclusions and uncertainty. Separate measured backscatter differences, inferred surface water and independently validated flood events. Never invent flood labels, confidence, accuracy, area, ground truth, interviews or agency partnerships. Unknowns remain unknown.

Preserve genuine originals/checksums and product maturity/version; do not silently normalize, repair metadata, filter or relabel science. Keep visualization transformations separate and documented. Reference labels have their own temporal, cloud, resolution and registration errors; publish limitations as prominently as results.

AI may assist writing/coding during authorized event work under the [global FAQ](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/) (accessed 2026-10-18), which requires disclosure of how it was used. AI does not authorize pre-event implementation. Keep an assistance log: tool, purpose, affected artifacts, human checks and unresolved errors. This preparation session used an AI assistant for documentation/research organization and terminal audits; it produced no competition model or application. AI text must not fabricate citations, scientific conclusions or validation. Verify every measurement against real outputs and every external claim against its source.

Never send Earthdata credentials, signed URLs, private data or imagery to an external AI service merely to process it. Use evidence-grounded factual summaries; optional generative explanation is not needed for MVP. Human review must catch unsupported causal language and safety implications.

Cite NASA–ISRO NISAR / ASF DAAC and exact dataset identifiers; observe third-party licenses and agency brand restrictions. Apache-2.0 covers original repository material, not all referenced content. Retain honest timestamps and prior-work disclosures. Eligibility, scientific integrity and participant privacy take priority over a polished demonstration.
