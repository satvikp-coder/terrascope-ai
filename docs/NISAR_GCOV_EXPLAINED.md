# Measurement dictionary

NISAR is the joint NASA–ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar mission. Active microwave imaging supports day/night and cloud-obscured observations; vegetation penetration and response depend on wavelength and scene conditions.

GCOV means Geocoded Polarimetric Covariance: radar covariance measurements on a geographic grid. HDF5 stores nested measurement arrays, identification, calibration, coordinate, and quality metadata. HHHH is the HH polarization autocovariance, related to radar intensity. Inspect actual units, normalization, fill values, and calibration before interpretation.

PROVISIONAL is calibrated maturity with remaining validation caveats; PR in a filename means Production processing, not provisional. Cycle, relative track, direction, frame, mode and polarization fields are separate. A 12-day interval does not prove comparability.

Reference: https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gcov/ ; https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/asf-search/
