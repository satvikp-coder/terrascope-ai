# User needs and existing solutions — desk research

Reviewed 2026-10-18. No interviews, adoption commitments or testimonials exist. Needs below are hypotheses drawn from published tool workflows and scientific literature, not validated customer requirements.

| Potential user | Problem worth investigating | Demonstration evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| GIS / remote-sensing analyst | Trace dates, geometry, units and QA before interpreting a change | Source IDs, quality exclusions and uncertainty visible alongside imagery |
| Flood researcher | Separate water evidence from artifacts and environmental confounders | Dated independent references and a reproducible assessment |
| Environmental researcher / educator | Understand what radar measures without treating every difference as an event | Plain-language physical explanations with evidence and limitations |
| Disaster-management analyst | Rapidly assess whether an observation is relevant and trustworthy | Clear timing/coverage/reliability; research prototype explicitly not emergency guidance |

| Existing tool / official source | What it offers | What we may investigate; not a claim the tool lacks all such capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| [ASF Vertex](https://search.asf.alaska.edu/) | SAR discovery, footprints, metadata and access | Simplifying provenance and two-date scientific context for newcomers |
| [NASA Worldview](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/worldview) | Broad Earth-observation visualization and date exploration | Linking a small study to explicit QA and validated interpretation |
| [Copernicus Browser](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data) | Optical/radar discovery and visualization | Explaining independent-source timing/cloud/geometry limitations |
| [JRC Surface Water Explorer](https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/) | Historical water occurrence/seasonality views | Distinguishing historical baseline from a particular new event |
