## Physical interpretation to learn before choosing a method

SAR actively measures microwave scattering; clouds do not create the optical visibility problem, but radar measurements retain geometry/noise/scattering ambiguities. Longer L-band wavelengths can interact differently with vegetation than C-band; penetration and flooded-vegetation response depend on canopy, structure, water and geometry. Smooth open water often scatters energy away from the sensor; wind/roughness can increase return, while flooded vegetation may brighten through double bounce. Neither darkness nor brightness is a universal flood label.

Gamma-0 and sigma-0 use different reference-area normalizations. The GCOV guide describes area-based terrain normalization; do not assume a simple flat-Earth angle formula substitutes for the supplied gamma-to-sigma factor. Our HHHH diagonal is real linear power, not amplitude or dB; 10 m spacing is not an independent 10 m resolution guarantee. No conversion, filtering or classification was done in this review.

Speckle is a scattering/statistical effect, distinct from additive instrument noise; numberOfLooks is not confidence. Temporal differences can reflect moisture, crops, vegetation, channel migration, roughness, geometry or calibration. RTC does not eliminate layover/shadow or prove physical registration. Matching grids and a 12-day separation are necessary context, not proof of comparable signal or flooding.

Supervised methods need representative independent labels; an unsupervised threshold still needs validation. Assess temporal comparability and artifacts before selecting either. Keep reference uncertainty, class imbalance and spatial dependence in the evaluation design. No threshold, model or target accuracy has been selected or implemented.

## Authoritative NISAR interpretation

- [NASA/ASF GCOV guide](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gcov/): RTC gamma-0 covariance terms, adaptive looks and normalization factor.
- [Provisional known issues](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/provisional-known-issues/): limited validation, residual artifacts and joint-mode 40 MHz banding. HHHH is unaffected by the documented off-diagonal polarimetric phase adjustment; this does not remove other radiometric uncertainty.
- [Product specification via GCOV guide](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gcov/): use version-matched units/masks/flags.

These established concepts motivate future tests; our proposed Majuli analysis remains an untested idea. External citation metadata is archived under 03_Documentation/research_20261018, outside Git.
